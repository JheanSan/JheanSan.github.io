Student's Thinking Machine Takes Best Project

SmartCity Bogotá answers questions about the city with real data, draws the answer on the map, and listens when you speak to it.

Asked where thefts gather, the machine does not guess. It checks local rules, then server templates, and only then hands the question to a language model that consults the city's own data.

Built by a team of three at the Universidad de San Buenaventura and deployed on Azure, it runs in the browser, as an installable app and on Android.
